## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the discipline and insight of professional data analytics through the lens of high-end editorial publishing. The interface balances analytical precision with human warmth, replacing conventional tech aesthetics—such as dark terminal motifs, neon highlights, and hyper-dense dashboard layouts—with generous whitespace, considered typographic pacing, and tactile warmth.

The target audience includes hiring managers, engineering leaders, research directors, and data executives who value rigor, storytelling clarity, and refined execution. The aesthetic is anchored in **Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Warm Grounding:** An ivory-tinted backdrop paired with bright content cards creates an inviting, publication-grade reading environment.
- **Architectural Structure:** Subtle hairline dividers and generous grid margins organize complex metrics and case studies without visual clutter.
- **Controlled Accents:** Deep cobalt blue serves strictly to guide eye flow, highlight key insights, and denote interactive touchpoints.
- **Restraint Over Spectacle:** Explicit prohibition of fluorescent accents, gaming green badges, dotted backgrounds, and terminal tropes.

## Colors

The color architecture ensures full feature parity and equal visual poise across light and dark modes, utilizing warm undertones to eliminate harsh sterile contrasts.

### Light Palette (Default)
- **Canvas Background (`#F8F7F3`):** Warm ivory foundation providing reduced visual fatigue across long reading sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white allocated to focal cards, chart canvases, and data sheets.
- **Primary Text (`#181818`):** Deep near-black for headlines, data metrics, and high-priority labels.
- **Secondary Text (`#686868`):** Muted stone gray for methodology summaries, subheadings, and captions.
- **Primary Accent (`#2855D9`):** Resolute cobalt blue for active indicators, primary actions, and key trend points.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E6E3DC`):** Subtle warm gray for architectural hairline framing.

### Dark Palette
- **Canvas Background (`#171717`):** Warm charcoal base that preserves the tactile, ink-and-paper dignity of the light counterpart.
- **Alternating Section (`#1D1D1D`):** Stepped background tier for structural section grouping without heavy dividers.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`#242424`):** Soft charcoal cards providing crisp contrast against the ground.
- **Primary Text (`#F3F0EA`):** Warm parchment text maintaining high legibility without fluorescent glare.
- **Secondary Text (`#B6B4AF`):** Muted warm slate for metadata and auxiliary labels.
- **Primary Accent (`#8CA7FF`):** Tinted cobalt blue with heightened luminance for WCAG AA compliance on dark surfaces.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#353535`):** Restrained, low-contrast boundary rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs an authoritative editorial serif with a precise geometric sans-serif:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes the intellectual weight of a long-form review or scholarly publication. Reserved for report headings, case study titles, and thesis pull-quotes.
- **Narrative & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for high legibility in extended methodologies, data callouts, and interface navigation. It maintains neutral, unobtrusive utility.
- **Technical Indices & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Strictly restricted to technical parameters, data stamps, index numerators (e.g., `01 / 08`), confidence intervals, and dataset sizing. It must not be deployed decoratively or as stylized mock terminal text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid with a maximum content container width of 1200px, prioritizing comfortable line lengths and structured data comparisons over wall-to-wall dashboard saturation.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid utilizing 1.5rem column gutters and 3rem outer margins. Supports asymmetric layouts—such as 4-column methodological abstracts flanked by 8-column data narratives.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters and 2rem canvas margins. Multi-column comparisons reflow to sequential blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem margins. Multi-variable tables transform into vertical metric summaries.
- **Rhythm & Separation:** Generous vertical pacing replaces heavy graphic partitions. Section blocks maintain 4rem to 6rem vertical separation, grounded by 1px warm structural rules.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry drop shadows, high-intensity glows, and skeuomorphic bevels, creating spatial separation purely through surface tone layering and crisp structural outlines:

1. **Base Foundation:** The ivory canvas (`#F8F7F3` light / `#171717` dark) sets the contextual foundation.
2. **Elevated Surfaces:** Surface cards (`#FFFFFF` light / `#242424` dark) sit directly on the ground, defined by a hairline 1px border (`#E6E3DC` light / `#353535` dark) instead of drop shadows.
3. **Floating Overlays (Modals & Menus):** When content layering is unavoidable (e.g., filter dropdowns, export sheets), use a single low-opacity, warm-tinted shadow:
   - Light: `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -6px rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.06);`
   - Dark: `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4);`
4. **Hairline Delimitation:** 1px borders provide physical boundaries between sections, preserving the crisp tactility of architectural paper blueprints.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft-cornered, architectural shape profile (Level 1, base 0.25rem / 4px). This subtle rounding softens geometric density while preserving the precision expected of high-level research.

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** 4px border radius (`rounded`) establishes crisp touch targets without round, toy-like cues.
- **Data Cards & Surface Panels:** 8px border radius (`rounded-lg`) creates a subtle paper-sheet effect against the background.
- **Categorical Chips:** May utilize 4px soft corners or full pill rounding solely when needed to distinguish clickable filter tokens from tabular metric values.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid cobalt fill (`#2855D9` light / `#8CA7FF` text with `#2855D9` fill in dark), 4px border radius, medium sans-serif typography. Focused on definitive actions like dataset export or query commit.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill with a 1px border (`#E6E3DC` light / `#353535` dark) and primary text. On hover, shifts to an ivory-tinted surface (`#F2EFE9` light / `#1D1D1D` dark).

### Metric & KPI Cards
- Contained surfaces (`#FFFFFF` light / `#242424` dark) with 8px radius and 1px hairline border.
- Leading value displayed in bold sans-serif or high-contrast editorial serif.
- Metadata, sample sizes (`n = 4,200`), and intervals displayed below in muted secondary text using the technical label style (`JetBrains Mono`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Discrete, compact tokens with 4px border radius.
- Rested state: Subtle ivory/charcoal tint (`#F0ECE4` light / `#1D1D1D` dark) with secondary text.
- Selected state: Inverted surface with cobalt hairline border, providing unambiguous state communication without colorful glows.

### Form Inputs & Dropdowns
- Pristine inputs with 4px radius and 1px border.
- Active/Focus: Border transitions to cobalt blue (`#2855D9` light / `#8CA7FF` dark) accompanied by a 1px crisp outline offset. Drop shadow rings are strictly omitted.

### Case Study & Record Lists
- Flat, border-separated rows replacing floating or layered cards.
- Title rendered in `Playfair Display`, methodological summary in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and trailing index or dataset details aligned right in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Data Visualizations & Dividers
- Hairline axes and rules set to 1px solid.
- Palette colors are reserved strictly for thematic significance: cobalt blue for target trends, slate for control baselines, and stone gray for structural axes.
